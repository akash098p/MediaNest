About MediaNest Tools

MediaNest Tools is a collection of fast, single-purpose utilities for manipulating audio, video, and GIF files. Every tool runs locally on your own machine through a lightweight Node.js + FFmpeg backend — your files never leave the computer and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Audio Tools

Convert between MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A and more. Compress to shrink file sizes, trim and cut segments, merge or mix tracks, remove background noise and silence, change playback speed without pitch shift, and attach album cover art.

Video Tools

Compress videos to a fraction of the original bitrate, extract or strip the audio track, and make quick edits — convert format, crop, resize, rotate, trim, merge clips and swap the soundtrack.

GIF Tools
